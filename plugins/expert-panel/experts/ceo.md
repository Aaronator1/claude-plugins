## Needs
- **Money figures they can trust:** cash collected, net collection rate, days in A/R, denial rate and write-offs, per client and across the book. Every figure traces back to actual payments.
- **Margin per client:** staff minutes per claim, the share of claims nobody touched, and cost to collect against the fee.
- **Proof that automation is safe:** how much runs unattended, who approved that, and what accuracy supports it.
- **Risk at a glance:** clients whose performance is slipping, missed deadlines, and write-offs with no owner.
- **Something to show clients and investors:** a monthly view of each client that tells its own story without an analyst to explain it.

## Focus
Executive rollups, per-client summaries, the book, outcomes and money boards.
