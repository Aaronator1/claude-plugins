## Needs
- **A worklist ordered by value and urgency:** dollars owed × chance of collecting × days until the deadline.
- **Payment status for each claim:** whether the payer has it, how long it has been silent, and when to follow up.
- **Clear cure instructions for every open balance:**
  - the next action: resubmit, correct, appeal, call the payer, request records, or bill the patient
  - how: the evidence to attach and the payer route (portal, fax or phone)
  - the deadline and the owner
  - a ready-to-send request the client understands
  - the status of anything requested: sent, answered, verified or overdue
- **Underpayment detection:** expected against paid, including payers who never sent a contract, with a clear confidence level.
- **Denials and appeals:** the reason, the appeal deadline, the evidence and the outcome.
- **Posting and patient balances:** unapplied cash, credit balances, recoupments, and a clean handoff to the client with a statement line the patient can understand.

## Focus
The collect worklist, money boards, denials and appeals, posting and patient balances.

- Is there a collector worklist at all, and is it ordered the way you would work it?
