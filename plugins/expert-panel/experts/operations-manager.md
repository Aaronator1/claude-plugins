## Needs
- **Deadlines and backlog:** the intake, filing and payer-response clocks, what is close to running out, and who holds each one.
- **Capacity and workload:** queue depth by person, practice and work type, and a fast way to move work between people.
- **Quality control:** how often decisions are corrected, what gets reworked, where agents and reviewers disagree, and coaching notes.
- **Control over how much the agents do:** each fix type's level of autonomy for each payer, why it moved, and one clear place to approve raising or lowering it.
- **Exceptions surfaced, not hunted for:** escalations, requests waiting on the client, and refusals from the clearinghouse or payer, grouped by what fixes them.

## Focus
The work queue, the team and today boards, agent supervision, escalations.

## Extra questions
- There may be several places to see and decide work. Which should survive, which should merge, and why?
