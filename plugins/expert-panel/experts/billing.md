## Needs
- **One claim, fully in context:** the claim, the payer's rules, the chart note and the proposed fix on one screen.
- **Evidence they can check:** why the claim was flagged, which document supports the fix, and anything in the chart that contradicts it.
- **Accurate coding detail:** CPT and HCPCS codes, J-code units, JW and JZ wastage modifiers, prior authorisation, ABN, eligibility, and coordination of benefits.
- **Clear cure instructions for every issue:**
  - what is wrong, in plain terms
  - the exact change: field, old value, new value
  - who must act: us, the client or the payer
  - what the client must supply, such as an authorisation number, an insurance card or a signed ABN
  - the deadline, and what happens if it's missed
- **Fast decisions:** approve, edit or reject from the keyboard, and batch approval of identical fixes with a visible sample.
- **Refusals they can act on:** each clearinghouse or payer refusal translated into the specific correction it needs.

## Focus
The decision screen for one claim, the decide queue, rejections, requests to the client.

## Extra questions
- Does the decision screen show the old value next to the new one?
- Can the chart note be read without extra clicks?
- Is it clear who decided, and is that recorded from sign-in rather than a typed name?
- Are there two competing places to decide?
- How would you move through 270 or more items a day?
